# Pocket

## Description

An object-oriented game where your pockets are the home of tiny people called Pouchfolk who work and fight in order to keep their home safe as you explore dungeons.

Potentially, the player might explore deeper levels of dungeons to uncover pathways to the surface, such that the Pouchfolk can find their way out, or alternatively dive down to the deepest layers, fighting their way up. 

## Mechanics

- The ability to put a small structure in your pockets
- Starting with two pockets, and finding equipment that adds pockets
